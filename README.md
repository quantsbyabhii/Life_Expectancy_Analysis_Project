# Life Expectancy & Global Health Analysis Dashboard

An enterprise-grade **Power BI Dashboard** engineered to track, model, and analyze macro-level global health metrics, healthcare economics, and socio-economic demographic indicators across a **15-year timeline (2000–2015)**.

## 📌 Executive Summary
This analytical solution uncovers the structural, financial, and biological drivers behind global human longevity. By synthesizing multidimensional datasets from global health entities, the dashboard equips policymakers and public health analysts with data-driven evidence to map disease vectors, identify systemic immunization gaps, and measure the ROI of nationwide health expenditures.

## 📊 Core Analytical Frameworks & Visualizations

### 1. High-Level Macro KPIs
* **Global Scale Monitoring:** Evaluates an aggregate sample size of **24 Billion** across tracked intervals to establish universal statistical baselines.
* **Longevity Benchmark:** Identifies the benchmark for average global life expectancy at **69.30 years**.
* **Mortality Control Index:** Captures the universal baseline for infant deaths at an average index score of **32.55**.

### 2. Healthcare Economics & Spending Efficiency
* **Status-Based Resource Allocation:** Investigates financial structural disparities, demonstrating that **Developing Nations** absorb **82.69%** of the analyzed population metrics yet suffer distinct infrastructure challenges compared to **Developed Nations (17.31%)**.
* **Outcomes vs. Expenditures:** Contextualizes how funding allocations correlate directly with structural longevity benchmarks.

### 3. Epidemiological & Immunization Disparity Mapping
* **Pathogen Prevalence Tracking:** Features a dynamic **Global GIS Heatmap** detailing spatial density layers for **HIV/AIDS transmission rates** across major landmasses.
* **Prophylactic Disparity Breakdown:** Compares clinical protection variations between developed and developing economic statuses for three critical vaccines:
  * **Polio (Poliomyelitis):** Contrasts a 94.49% developed baseline against lower developing averages.
  * **Hepatitis B:** Identifies immunization drops down to a 77.73% operational floor in lower-income statuses.
  * **Diphtheria:** Visualizes the systemic drop from a 94.64% operational baseline down to 82.35%.

### 4. Longitudinal Demographic & Nutritional Trends
* **Inverse Co-Integration Analysis:** Models the strong historical negative correlation between declining **Infant Mortality Rates** and ascending **Life Expectancy** from 2000 to 2015.
* **Malnutrition & Growth Co-Modeling:** Plots dual-axis timelines detailing population scaling alongside strict pediatric malnutrition markers (**Thinness 1–9 years** and **Thinness 1–19 years**).

## 🛠️ Data Engineering & Architecture
* **Data Integration:** Cleaned, structured, and normalized sparse historical metrics from global development reporting systems using **Power Query**.
* **Data Modeling:** Constructed a star-schema analytical model optimized for low-latency filtering across geography, timeline parameters, and economic development tiers.
* **Advanced Aggregations (DAX):** Developed optimized measures for weighted rolling averages, cross-categorical distribution ratios, and dynamic multi-year trend baselines.
